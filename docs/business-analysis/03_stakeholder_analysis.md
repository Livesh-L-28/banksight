# Stakeholder Analysis
## Project Name: BankSight — Banking Customer Intelligence & Retention Platform
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Date:** 2026-11-07  

---

## 1. Stakeholder Matrix

| Stakeholder Persona | Organizational Role | Core Responsibilities | Key Interests & Success Metrics | Level of Influence | Level of Interest |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Executive Leadership** | Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Chief Risk Officer (CRO), Head of Retail Banking | Strategic oversight, capital allocation, shareholder value, brand health | Portfolio churn reduction, Net Interest Margin (NIM) protection, total deposit growth, cost of acquisition | High | High |
| **Retention Manager** | Head of Customer Retention & Loyalty | Managing operational retention campaigns, retention queue assignments, incentive budgets | Accurate churn risk classification, retention conversion rates, time-to-contact SLA, saved customer value | High | High |
| **Relationship Manager (RM)** | Branch & Private Banking Relationship Manager | High-touch customer engagement, annual account reviews, direct client outreach | Actionable client 360 dossiers, clear churn drivers, personalized incentive recommendations | Medium | High |
| **Branch Manager** | Branch Operations & Sales Lead | Branch profitability, customer acquisition, local footfall, deposit volume | Branch ranking, regional benchmarking, dormant customer activation, deposit outflow alerts | Medium | High |
| **Marketing Campaign Manager** | Head of Product Marketing & CRM | Planning customer acquisition and cross-sell campaigns, marketing ROI | Segment reach, campaign conversion rate, CAC, multi-touch revenue attribution | Medium | High |
| **Banking Business Analyst** | Retail Strategy & Analytics Specialist | Financial modeling, cohort analysis, ad-hoc management reports | Self-service slicing, validated KPI definitions, reliable export options (Excel/PDF) | Medium | High |
| **Data & ML Engineer** | Analytics & Architecture Lead | Pipeline stability, model accuracy, database integrity, CI/CD | Model reproducibility, absence of data leakage, automated data quality validation | High | Medium |
| **Compliance & Risk Officer** | Regulatory & Ethics Officer | Fair lending compliance, explainable AI, customer data privacy | Model explainability (SHAP/Feature importance), synthetic data privacy, advisory human-in-the-loop controls | High | Medium |

---

## 2. Stakeholder Engagement & Governance Plan
1. **Weekly Retention Review:** Retention Managers and RMs review the high-risk customer queue and log outcomes.
2. **Monthly Branch Benchmarking:** Branch Managers review regional rankings, deposit variance, and customer migration trends.
3. **Quarterly Executive SteerCo:** Executive Leadership evaluates macro churn trends, marketing campaign ROI, and model performance drifts.
