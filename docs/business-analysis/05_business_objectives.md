# Business Objectives & Success Metrics
## Project Name: BankSight — Banking Customer Intelligence & Retention Platform
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Date:** 2026-11-07  

---

## 1. Primary Business Objectives

1. **Reduce Portfolio Attrition Rate:**
   - Reduce annualized customer churn rate by 300 to 500 basis points (e.g. from 18.5% down to 14.5% or lower) across targeted retail banking cohorts within 12 months.
   - Establish automated early warning scoring with lead time of 60 to 90 days prior to account closure or balance depletion.

2. **Increase Retention Campaign Effectiveness:**
   - Achieve at least a 35% conversion rate on high-risk retention interventions managed through the platform.
   - Reduce average time-to-contact from 14 days down to under 48 hours for high-risk accounts.

3. **Improve Customer Lifetime Value (CLV):**
   - Increase multi-product penetration from 1.6 products per customer to 2.4 products per customer across core accounts via intelligent cross-sell recommendations.
   - Preserve at least $25M in retail customer deposits annually through preemptive retention actions.

4. **Enhance Analytical Operational Efficiency:**
   - Reduce time spent compiling monthly branch and product intelligence reports from 80 analyst hours per month to automated real-time generation (< 30 seconds).
   - Empower business and branch users with 100% self-service drill-down into underlying transaction and customer records.

5. **Govern AI Ethics & Explainability:**
   - 100% of churn predictions must feature explainable factor breakdowns (top contributing features) and calibrated probabilities before operational display.
   - Maintain zero data leakage in predictive model training pipelines.
