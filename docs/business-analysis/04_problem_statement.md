# Problem Statement
## Project Name: BankSight — Banking Customer Intelligence & Retention Platform
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Date:** 2026-11-07  

---

## 1. Background & Context
Retail and commercial banks operate in an increasingly commoditized environment characterized by frictionless digital account switching, rising customer acquisition costs, and intensifying competition from fintech challengers. Historically, customer defection has been addressed reactively—often only after an account has been closed, direct deposits redirected, or balances reduced to zero.

## 2. Core Problem Statements

### Problem 1: Inability to Detect Early Attrition Signals
Retail banks fail to detect early attrition signals (such as declining transaction frequency, decreased digital logins, unaddressed customer complaints, or reduced deposit balances) until the customer has already decided to defect. By the time account closure paperwork is filed, the customer is virtually unrecoverable.

### Problem 2: Fragmented Customer Visibility (Siloed Data)
Customer data resides across disparate silos: core banking systems (checking/savings), loan servicing platforms, credit card processors, branch logs, and customer service desks. Relationship managers lack a single, consolidated "Customer 360" view displaying demographic attributes, financial holdings, and recent interaction timelines.

### Problem 3: Rudimentary and Static Customer Segmentation
Existing customer segmentation relies on crude, static demographic bands or arbitrary account balance thresholds. Banks lack behavioral clustering and automated RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analytics that capture dynamic changes in transaction cadence, engagement patterns, and lifetime value potential.

### Problem 4: Opaque and Unexplainable AI Models
Where machine learning churn models exist, they often operate as unexplainable "black boxes." Front-line Relationship Managers cannot discern why a customer was flagged as high-risk, leading to low model trust, inappropriate retention incentives, and wasted budget.

### Problem 5: Lack of Closed-Loop Retention Workflow
Even when risk lists are generated, they are circulated as disconnected spreadsheets with no centralized workflow tracking. Management cannot track who contacted which customer, which offers were proposed, or whether retention interventions actually prevented attrition.

### Problem 6: Inaccurate Campaign & Channel Attribution
Marketing teams run campaigns without robust baseline controls or attribution modeling, leading to misleading ROI claims and misallocated marketing capital.

---

## 3. Desired Future State
BankSight solves these fundamental challenges by providing a unified, explainable, closed-loop banking intelligence and retention platform that integrates multi-tier predictive modeling directly with operational case management, deep customer 360 dossiers, branch benchmarking, and auditable reporting.
